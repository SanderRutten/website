## Comments!
Although I don't expect anyone to do so, it is now possible to leave a comment on my posts!

The [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) theme I'm using is [Stack](https://github.com/CaiJimmy/hugo-theme-stack). This theme supports many comment providers. Although some projects seem to be death or very inactive, while others are paid/ad supported. Or even worse, they require a login.

Not the case with [remark42](https://remark42.com). It is running on my own server, doesn't require a login, and no tracking or whatsoever.<br>
I could support logins at various social services, but I did not enable those. Anonymous "login" only. Or E-Mail login, but that is mainly for me :)
